Why ClearCut?

🔒

100% Private

Images are processed entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored on any server.

💎

No Quality Loss

Output is a full-resolution transparent PNG. No compression, no watermarks, no downscaling.

🆓

Completely Free

No accounts, no credits, no limits. Powered by open-source AI that runs on your device.

⚡

Fast & Offline

After the first model download, it works offline. WebGPU accelerates when available.

Privacy First

ClearCut uses the open-source @imgly/background-removal library. The AI model (ISNet) downloads once and is cached by your browser. Your photos never leave your device — not even for a millisecond.
